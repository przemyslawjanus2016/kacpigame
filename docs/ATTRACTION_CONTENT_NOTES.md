# Content notes for attraction cards

The attraction texts are original, child-friendly summaries written for the app (PL/EN), not copied from source pages.

Reference families used while curating facts:
- Wieliczka Salt Mine / Kraków Saltworks Museum: history of salt mining, chambers and castle.
- Official Kraków tourism and heritage materials: Main Market Square, Wawel, Planty, city fortifications and Vistula boulevards.
- Tatra National Park and public geographic references: trails, protected wildlife, mountain safety and landmark descriptions.
- Wikimedia Commons file descriptions for photo-specific details (for example the Copernicus chamber, Staszic Chamber and the underground brine lake).

For a store release, factual copy should receive a final editorial review together with the photo-license audit.
